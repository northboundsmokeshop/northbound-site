Your Timberlea vape & smoke shop

If you live north of the river, this is your closest Northbound. Grab replacement coils and pods, try a new device with help from staff who use the gear themselves, or stock up on rolling papers, lighters and smoke shop accessories.

Call (780) 750-0750 to check stock before you come in. If we're out of something, we'll check our Downtown and Thickwood stores for you. All ages are welcome in the store. Vapes, e-liquids and tobacco are sold only to customers 18 and older, with valid government photo ID.
