Your Thickwood vape & smoke shop

The Thickwood team stocks starter kits, refillable pod systems, box mods and all the parts that keep them running: coils, pods, tanks, batteries and chargers. Not sure which coil fits your device? Bring it in and we'll match it.

Call (780) 750-0078 to check stock or set an order aside for pickup. All ages are welcome in the store. Vapes, e-liquids and tobacco are sold only to customers 18 and older, with valid government photo ID.
