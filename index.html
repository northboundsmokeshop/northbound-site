Questions

Where is the closest vape shop in Fort McMurray?

Northbound has three Fort McMurray locations: Downtown at 9613 Franklin Ave, Timberlea at 108 Riverstone Ridge #115 and Thickwood at 101 Signal Rd #37.

What are your hours?

All three Northbound locations are open 7 days a week, 9 AM to 9:30 PM. Timberlea and Thickwood stay open until 10 PM on Fridays and Saturdays.

How old do I need to be?

To buy vapes, e-liquids or tobacco, you must be 18 or older in Alberta. We check government-issued photo ID for anyone who looks under 25. Our Timberlea and Thickwood stores welcome all ages; Downtown is 18+ only.

Can I call ahead and pick up in store?

Yes. Call the location nearest you to check stock and we'll set your order aside.

Do you sell coils, pods and replacement parts?

Yes. Every location stocks coils, pods, tanks, batteries and chargers for the major device brands.

Where can I buy premium cigars in Fort McMurray?

Northbound has a walk-in humidor with premium Cuban and New World cigars, plus cutters, cases and desktop humidors. Call your nearest store to check what's in stock.
