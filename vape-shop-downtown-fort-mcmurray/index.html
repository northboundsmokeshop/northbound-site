Your Downtown vape & smoke shop

Drop in on your lunch break, on the way home from work or before heading out on Highway 63. The Downtown team helps with first-time setups, swaps out coils and pods for the major device brands and keeps a full wall of e-liquids in a range of nicotine strengths.

In a hurry? Call (780) 750-0950, tell us what you need and we'll have it ready at the counter. Downtown is an 18+ store: you must be 18 or older to enter, and we check valid government photo ID.

Getting here

On Franklin Ave in the Downtown core. From Beacon Hill, Abasand or Gregoire, take Highway 63 north to the Downtown exits and follow Franklin Ave to the 9600 block.
