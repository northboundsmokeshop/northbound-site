About Northbound

A Fort McMurray vape and smoke shop with three stores across town.

Northbound Specialty Smoke and Vape Shop is locally owned and operated in Fort McMurray, Alberta. We run three stores, Downtown on Franklin Ave, in Timberlea at Riverstone Ridge and in Thickwood on Signal Rd, so there's always one close to home or work.

Our staff use the products they sell. They can help you pick a first device, find the right replacement coil or pod for the one you already have, and answer questions about nicotine strengths.

How we sell

Vaping and tobacco products are for adults. We sell them only to customers 18 and older, and we check government-issued photo ID for anyone who looks under 25. Our Timberlea and Thickwood stores welcome all ages; Downtown is 18+ only. Every e-liquid we carry complies with Health Canada's rules on nicotine concentration and packaging.
